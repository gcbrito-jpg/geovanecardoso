Research

My research is in spectral geometry and geometric analysis. I study eigenvalue multiplicities for Laplace-type operators in different geometric settings. In particular, I am interested in whether the spectrum is simple for a generic choice of geometric parameters.

I also study the geometry of Wasserstein spaces and its relation to elliptic operators on the underlying Riemannian manifold. My other interests include Lie groups and homogeneous spaces.
