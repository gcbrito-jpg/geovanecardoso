Geovane Cardoso de Brito

Postdoctoral researcher in Mathematics
IMECC · University of Campinas

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Statistics and Scientific Computing, University of Campinas, working with Christian S. Rodrigues.

I completed my PhD at the University of São Paulo, advised by Marcone Corrêa Pereira and co-advised by Marcus A. M. Marrocos.

My interests lie in spectral geometry and geometric analysis, particularly Laplace-type operators, optimal transport, and the geometry of Wasserstein spaces.
